## Project Overview

Understanding where sales and profit come from is essential for making better business decisions. This project analyzes Superstore sales data to evaluate revenue performance, profitability, product categories, and regional sales patterns.

The analysis transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights using data cleaning, DAX, and interactive Power BI visualizations. It focuses on identifying the products, categories, and regions driving business performance, as well as areas where sales do not translate into strong profitability.

The project goes beyond reporting sales figures by examining the relationship between sales and profit to highlight opportunities for improving overall business performance.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Cleaning
- Data Transformation
- Data Visualization
- Dashboard Design

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Analyze regional sales and profitability
- Identify products and categories with low or negative profit
- Compare sales performance across different segments
- Identify key trends and business opportunities
- Present findings through an interactive dashboard

## Key Insights

- The West region generates the highest sales performance.
- Technology is the strongest-performing category in terms of profit.
- Some categories, including Bookcases and Tables, generate losses despite contributing to sales.
- Sales volume does not always translate into higher profitability.
